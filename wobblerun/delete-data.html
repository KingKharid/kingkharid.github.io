Delete your data — Wobble Run

Publisher: Clickabble · Contact: clickabble@gmail.com

Wobble Run has no accounts and no servers holding your data. Everything the game saves stays on your device, so you can delete all of it yourself in a few seconds.

Delete everything (recommended)

Uninstall Wobble Run from your device. Android removes the app's private storage, including your level progress, stars, coins, unlocked skins, achievements and settings. Alternatively use Settings → Apps → Wobble Run → Storage → Clear data to remove the same data while keeping the app installed.

What is deleted

  • Level progress, star ratings and best times
  • Coin balance and unlocked cosmetic items
  • Achievement progress and daily-reward history
  • Settings, including chosen game mode and selected skin
  • Locally cached purchase entitlement markers (Google Play keeps the purchase itself, so reinstalling restores what you bought)

What is not held by us

We do not operate a backend. Advertising data processed by Google AdMob and payment data processed by Google Play Billing are handled by Google, not stored by Clickabble. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and review or request deletion of Google-held data through Google's data controls.

Request deletion by email

If you would like us to confirm in writing that nothing about you is held by Clickabble, or you have a question about a purchase, email clickabble@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We reply within 30 days. Because no account exists, we cannot identify you from an email address alone — the app stores nothing that links your device to us.
