Privacy Policy — Wobble Run

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Publisher: Clickabble · Contact: clickabble@gmail.com

Wobble Run ("the game") is designed to work offline and without an account. This policy explains what the game does with information.

Summary

  • The core game is fully playable offline and requires no account.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Progress (levels, stars, coins, cosmetics, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The published build includes two Google SDKs: Google AdMob (optional ads) and Google Play Billing (optional purchases). Both are described below.

Data stored on your device

Save data lives in the app's private storage: unlocked levels, star ratings, best times, coin balance, cosmetic unlocks, selected skin, achievement progress, daily-reward timestamp, purchase entitlements (if any) and settings. It is never transmitted anywhere and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for example a revive after a failed level) and a limited number of interstitial ads between levels. Interstitial ads never appear in the game's Little Wob mode for younger players.

To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier, IP address, and coarse device and app information, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This data is collected by Google as an independent controller, is not linked by us to any identity, and is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), the game asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can decline; the game remains fully playable. A one-time "Remove Ads" purchase disables interstitial ads, while reward ads you choose to watch stay available.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play returns a purchase token and order identifier which the game uses only to unlock the item you bought, to finish the transaction, and to restore purchases after reinstalling. Purchases sit behind a "grown-ups only" hold-to-confirm step. Purchase history is processed for app functionality and is not shared by us with third parties.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It contains no chat, no user-generated content, no social features and no external links other than platform store pages. The Little Wob mode intended for younger players shows no ads at all, and all purchases remain behind the parent gate.

Permissions

The published Android build requests only:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — required by the advertising and billing SDKs to load ads and contact Google Play.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — the Google advertising identifier used by AdMob.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — required for optional purchases.

The game does not request storage, camera, microphone, contacts or location access.

Your choices

  • Delete all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.
  • Decline ad consent where prompted; the game remains playable.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Disable in-app purchases at the device level (Play Store → Settings → require authentication for purchases).

Data retention and security

All gameplay data stays on your device. No server holds your progress in this version. If cloud features are added later, this policy will be updated before release.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date and noted in the store listing.
