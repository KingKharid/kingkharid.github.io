About the game

Meet Wob — a squishy little jelly with big feelings and even bigger bounce. Wob squashes when landing, stretches in mid-air and giggles when things go wrong, so every jump feels alive. Run, hop and bounce through four colourful worlds packed with springs, rolling boulders, swinging hammers and wobbly candy bridges.

  • 100 handcrafted levels: Candy Chaos, Crazy Jungle, Lava Land, Frozen Madness
  • Collect crystals for stars, beat target times, replay for three stars
  • Six jelly skins unlocked with coins you earn by playing
  • Daily rewards and achievements
  • Plays fully offline — no account, no internet needed
  • Cosmetics only. No energy timers, no pay-to-win

Little Wob mode

A gentler way to play for younger or less experienced players: forgiving physics, no fail state, and no advertising at all. It is a difficulty option available from the mode chooser, not a separate product.

Purchases and ads

The game is free to play and finishes without spending anything. Optional one-time purchases remove interstitial ads or unlock cosmetic skins; nothing is randomised and nothing is pay-to-win. Rewarded ads (such as an optional revive) are always opt-in, and purchases sit behind a "grown-ups only" hold-to-confirm step.
