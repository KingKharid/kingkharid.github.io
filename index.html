Wobble Run

A one-thumb jelly platformer. Bounce, wobble and squish through 100 handcrafted levels across four worlds, collect crystals for stars, and unlock jelly skins. Plays fully offline — no account, no energy timers, no pay-to-win.

  • 100 levels: Candy Chaos, Crazy Jungle, Lava Land, Frozen Madness
  • Little Wob mode: a gentler, ad-free way to play
  • Cosmetic unlocks only; nothing is gated behind an ad
